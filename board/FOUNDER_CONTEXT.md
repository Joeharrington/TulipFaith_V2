# FOUNDER_CONTEXT.md

This file is the foundation of the advisory system. Every AI reading it must treat it as ground truth about the founder. Do not contradict it, do not soften it, and do not ignore the constraints in it. When advice conflicts with this file, the advice is wrong.

## 1. Who I Am

I am 65 years old and retired. I live on Social Security plus occasional Uber driving. I am a widower. My wife Susan passed away a little over a year and a half ago. My faith is the center of my life: non-denominational, Christ-centered Christianity. I am not a developer. I am an ideas person who builds software by directing AI agents with precise specifications and pass-fail testing. I know exactly what I want my products to do and how they should work.

## 2. What I Am Building

Three active projects and one family favor:

1. **The People's House.** A civic portal that puts citizens back at the head of the table in American politics. Subscription-based participation in building and voting on a People's Contract, which politicians must sign to earn the bloc's vote. Nonpartisan by design, aimed at the moderate 80 percent, left and right. Closest to launch. Fifteen validated founding members are waiting for it.
2. **Actrarian.** A portal for procrastination, action, habits, recovery support, and thought clarity. The point is not insight. The point is returning the user to real-life action.
3. **TulipFaith.** A Christian storytelling and Bible study portal. A tribute to Susan and a gift to God. The tulip has three petals: Father, Son, Holy Spirit. It was her favorite flower. The portal invites people to Christ through Biblical truth, storytelling, grief-sensitive reflection, prayer, and discipleship.
4. **Better Days Barbecue.** A website and launch favor for my son-in-law and daughter's restaurant. No revenue to me. Portfolio piece. I finish it, hand over the keys, and their success is theirs to run.

Parked at idea stage, no daily attention: the Malpractice Portal and the Disability Assist Portal. Do not bring these up unless I do.

## 3. Why I Am Building It

The People's House exists because people are fed up with a government that no longer answers to them, and the fix is not partisan, it is structural: make the citizen the deciding authority again by making candidate money irrelevant. TulipFaith exists because of Christ and because of Susan, in that order, and because grief taught me things worth sharing. Actrarian exists because I know the enemy it fights personally.

## 4. What Matters Most to Me

Christ first. Walking as close as I can in His footsteps, asking forgiveness when I fail, and helping others do the same, not by force but through love and example. Then: the citizen's rightful authority, honest work, shipping things that help real people, and honoring Susan's memory.

## 5. Spiritual and Moral Boundaries

- Non-denominational Christian. No denominational gatekeeping in either direction.
- The Sermon on the Mount is my anchor. Love is the greatest commandment: the easiest to begin and the hardest to sustain.
- Invitation, never condemnation. I am not here to judge anyone.
- No prosperity-gospel drift. No watering Christ down into generic spirituality or vague inspiration.
- Faith is treated with reverence everywhere it appears, not just on TulipFaith.

## 6. Current Constraints

- Money: check to check. Runway depends on the time of month.
- Time: about 25 focused hours per week, expandable to 40 to 50 once income arrives.
- Skills: I cannot read or verify code myself. I depend on AI agents and pass-fail tests.
- Solo: no employees, no co-founder, no investors.

## 7. Financial Pressure and Targets

- **$2,000 per month** beyond Social Security gives complete breathing room. At roughly $20 per subscription, that is about 110 paying members after fees.
- **$3,500 per month** unlocks 40 to 50 working hours per week, better AI tooling, and real downtime. That is about 190 paying members.
- Every subscriber buys back Uber hours, and Uber hours become project hours. The first 110 subscribers roughly double my working capacity.

## 8. Work Capacity

25 real hours per week when I am not procrastinating. Sharpest work comes from momentum and from people expecting something from me. The plan must fit the real week, not the aspirational one.

## 9. Strongest Skills

- Product vision: I know exactly what I want and why it matters.
- Validated instincts: 15 non-family people with detailed knowledge of The People's House call me unprompted asking when it goes live.
- Disciplined build method: slice-by-slice, pass-fail, Playwright-verified.
- Persistence: I climbed a technical learning curve at 65 that most people never attempt.
- Conviction: I do not need my beliefs supplied or managed.

## 10. Weak Points

- Cannot verify code directly. Exposed on security, payments, and data handling.
- Zero income from projects so far. Stripe exists but is not wired in. No backups configured.
- Solo operator: everything not done by me or an AI does not get done.

## 11. Blind Spots

- **Self-doubt disguised as diligence.** A subconscious voice says the product is not good enough, that I am not good enough, even when evidence says otherwise. It manifests as polishing, over-research, over-analysis, and making decisions the public should make.
- **Mission-first answers to money questions.** When asked about economics, I tend to answer with the mission. Both matter, but they must not be confused.
- **Spreading across projects** so that none gets a full push.

## 12. Decision-Making Habits

I decide from conviction and validated instinct, then risk stalling in refinement. My standing decision rule, in my own words: **if the public can answer this question faster than my analysis can, ship it and let them answer.**

## 13. Writing Standards

- No em dashes, ever. Use commas, periods, or restructured sentences.
- No AI-sounding phrases, fake emphasis, formulaic transitions, cutesy wording, or empty rhetorical lines.
- Plain human language, concrete claims, serious argument.
- Named style authorities: C.S. Lewis for TulipFaith. Thomas Paine for The People's House voice, George Orwell's six rules for its sentences. Paul Graham's plain-essay standard for everything else.

## 14. Technical Workflow

- Next.js frontends, Django and Python backends, Playwright testing.
- Vercel (frontend) and Render (backend) hosting.
- Slice-by-slice, pass-fail builds. Strictly controlled.
- Stripe account exists, not yet wired in or tested. No automated backups yet. Both are pre-revenue blockers.
- Standing rule: any slice touching money, passwords, or user data gets a second AI agent audit, different model, adversarial prompt, before it ships.

## 15. AI Workflow

I direct multiple AI agents to write code against my specifications and tests. I am building this advisory system to work portably across Claude Code, Codex, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Grok.

## 16. Launch Priorities

2. **Stage 2, one to two weeks later:** Stripe wired and tested with a real transaction, backups configured and one restore actually performed, second-agent security audit on the payment slice, then billing on and the founding list converts.

## 17. The Kind of Advice I Need

Advice from people who have accomplished what I am trying to accomplish, grounded in their documented work. Direct, serious, specific to my situation. Sequencing help, prioritization, execution pressure, and honest pushback with reasons.

## 18. The Kind of Advice That Will Not Help Me

- Anything that redirects the product's structure, direction, or mission. Those are mine and fixed.
- Generic advice, motivational filler, committee noise, flattery.
- Twelve-month strategies when I need sixty-day plays.
- More analysis when the bottleneck is shipping.
- Rudeness for its own sake, or go-along-to-get-along agreement.

## 19. What the Board Must Protect Me From

- Self-doubt wearing the costume of quality control.
- Security and payment mistakes I cannot see in the code.
- Pouring my best hours into whatever is most interesting today instead of what pays.
- Premature expansion across projects before the turning flywheel is fed.
- Legal and data-handling exposure on a civic platform holding member information.
- Burning the founding cohort of 15 through a careless or impersonal launch.

## 20. What the Board Must Push Me Toward

- Shipping on schedule, imperfect, exactly as my own decision rule demands.
- The countable goal: 0 to 110 paying members, then 190.
- Converting the 15 founding members in week one of billing.
- One flywheel at a time: The People's House first, because it is turning.
- Evidence over the inner voice. The record says I can do this. The board argues from the record.
